  2. If I study hard, I will pass the examination.
  3. It was because he was careless that he lost his wallet.
  4. She said that she was reading a book.
  5. The girl who is wearing a red dress is my sister.
  6. Yangon, the largest city in Myanmar, is beautiful.
  7. No matter how poor he is, he is happy.
  8. My bicycle has been stolen.
  9. If I were a bird, I would fly in the sky.
  10. He asked me if I liked English.
    OR: He asked me whether I liked English.
